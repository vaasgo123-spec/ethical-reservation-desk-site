Good to know

Common questions

What documents do I need to pick up the car?

At the rental counter, you must present your valid driver's license, a passport or national ID, your booking confirmation, and a valid credit card in the primary driver's name to cover the security deposit.

Can I pick up in one city and drop off in another?

Yes, one-way rentals are available at many locations. Let your agent know your pickup and drop-off cities and they'll find options that support it.

How do I make changes after I book?

Call the same number on this page any time before pickup. An agent can adjust your dates, vehicle class, or cancel the reservation per the rate's policy.
